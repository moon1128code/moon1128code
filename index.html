02 / Things I've worked on
01

Financial text annotation & retrieval

A graduation thesis project exploring how rules and large language models can extract financial information from annual reports and support traceable question answering.

My contribution & approach
  • Designed a workflow covering PDF parsing, text chunking, annotation, database construction and RAG verification.
  • Implemented retrieval using SQLite FTS5, TF-IDF and BM25.
  • Used structured prompts and rule-based checks to support consistent outputs and page-level traceability.

Policy document question answering

An internship project focused on making policy documents easier to search and understand through retrieval-assisted answers.

My contribution & approach
